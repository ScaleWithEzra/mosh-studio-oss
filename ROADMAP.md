# Roadmap

Mosh Studio currently supports a clip timeline, six motion-vector effects,
mosh transitions, text, overlays, 480p previews, and 1080p exports.

Possible next improvements:

- Faster scrubbing and previews for longer projects.
- More transition controls and glitch effects.
- Better editing controls on smaller screens.
- More flexible audio mixing and fades.

These are ideas rather than scheduled releases. Contributions and bug reports
can help decide what comes next.
